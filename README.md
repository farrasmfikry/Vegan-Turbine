Vegan Turbine — Student Wind Energy Project

Nantes, France | 2025

Project Overview

The Vegan Turbine project was a student engineering competition focused on wind turbine design, blade manufacturing, material selection, and power performance. Our team explored natural-fibre composite materials as an alternative for small wind turbine blades and evaluated turbine performance through wind-tunnel testing.

My Contributions

Blade manufacturing: Participated in the manufacturing of small-scale wind turbine blades.

Material selection: Worked with flax-fibre composite materials and bioresin.

Experimental testing: Contributed to wind-tunnel testing and the evaluation of turbine performance.

Performance analysis: Worked with power-performance data and annual energy production (AEP) assessment.
